Our story

Three generations
behind the counter.

What began as a single market stall is now a fabric house the whole bazaar knows. We buy directly from mills we've traded with for decades — which is why our shelves hold cloth you won't find in the department stores.

Every bolt is inspected by hand before it earns a place on the shelf. If we wouldn't hang it in our own homes, we don't sell it in yours.

What we do

More than a fabric shop.

Custom curtains & drapes

Made-to-measure headings, linings and hems — measured in your home, stitched in our workroom, hung by our hands.
